What's already in your house

Bring the lights, locks and speakers you have. The hub finds them and asks which room each is in.

  • Lights and plugs
  • Switches and dimmers
  • Locks
  • Thermostats
  • Cameras and doorbells
  • Blinds and shades
  • Speakers and TVs
  • Motion and door sensors

Including Philips Hue, Sonos, Google Nest, Ring, ecobee, WiZ, TP-Link Kasa, Chromecast and Roku, Samsung, LG and Android TVs, anything that works with Matter, Zigbee or Z-Wave things with a USB radio, and most of what Home Assistant works with.

Ways to get it

On a computer you have

A Raspberry Pi 5, an Intel NUC or a mini PC running Linux. One command, free. Then open hub.local on a phone on the same Wi‑Fi.

curl -fsSL https://hub.elyir.com/install | sudo bash

On a new Raspberry Pi 5

Put the card in, plug the Pi into power and your router, and open hub.local on your phone.

Questions

Do I need to know Home Assistant?

No. The hub runs it for you, and nothing in the house ever asks you to open it.

What happens when the internet is down?

The house keeps working: the wall, the phones at home and the routines. Only using it away from home waits for the internet to come back.

What does it cost?

Nothing at home: the software is free, on a computer you have. Using it away from home is $3 a month, and stopping only stops that.

What leaves my house?

Nothing unless you ask for it. What leaves your house says exactly what does when you do.

Does it work with Apple Home or Google Home?

Yes. Share the house with either from the hub, and the lights you choose show up there too.

What screen goes on the wall?

Any tablet or touchscreen with a web browser. Every phone in the house gets the same house.

What if I move?

Leave the hub for the next people: your phones, passcode and sign-ins go, and the rooms, lights and routines stay for them. Or erase it and take it with you.
