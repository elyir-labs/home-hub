Ways to get it

On a computer you have

A Raspberry Pi 5, an Intel NUC or a mini PC running Linux. One command, free. Then open hub.local on a phone on the same Wi‑Fi.

curl -fsSL https://hub.elyir.com/install | sudo bash

On a new Raspberry Pi 5

Put the card in, plug the Pi into power and your router, and open hub.local on your phone.
