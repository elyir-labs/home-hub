## What's new

- A ceiling fan with a light in it is one tile now: the fan, with its light as a row on it.
- If you reach for that light more than the fan, its page has a choice under *Lead with*, and the light becomes the tile with the fan as the row.

## Details

The fan and its light used to be two unrelated tiles, sometimes in different columns of the same
room. They are one fixture on the ceiling and now one tile on the wall. The fan leads by default; tap
the light's row to switch it, hold it to open the light's own page with the dimmer.

Either part's page offers the choice of which leads, and the choice is the fixture's: pick *Light*
on one and both know. With the light leading it keeps the large tile and the dimmer under your
finger, and the fan is the row, saying Low, Medium or High.

Nothing changes in what the house does. Sleep and All off still turn the light off, "bedroom lights
off" still reaches it, and "fan on" still means the fan alone.
