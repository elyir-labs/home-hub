## What's new

- A fridge's ice maker, a dishwasher's settings and the like show as part of their appliance, on one card, and stay on when everything else is turned off.
- A switch with a motion sensor built in is one thing now: one line when it is new and asks for a room, and one tile that says when it sees someone.
- Rename anything, or move it to another room, from its own page.

## Details

Some appliances show their settings to the hub as plain switches, and until now the hub treated them
as plugs: an ice maker went off with the plugs when the house emptied, and "turn off the plugs"
reached it. The hub now recognizes a switch that is part of a fridge, freezer, dishwasher, washer,
dryer, oven, water heater, pool or spa from its name, and shows it as an appliance instead. That
means Everything off and Bedtime walk past it, the row of what is on right now does not list a thing
that is on all year, and a machine with two or more such settings is one card with a row for each.
Where the hub guesses wrong, *Show this as* on the thing's own page puts it right either way, and the
line under the choice says the difference: a plug goes off when the house empties, an appliance is
left alone.

A switch with a motion sensor in it used to arrive as two things, each asking which room it lived in,
with nothing to say they were one. It is one line now, its parts named under it, and it takes one
answer. In its room, the switch's tile says *Motion* while its sensor sees someone, and the sensor no
longer has a separate chip of its own. Renaming the switch renames the sensor with it.

*Rename or move it* on a thing's own page used to open the hub's settings, where there was no rename
to be found. It now turns the name and the room into a field and a picker right there. The name
fields in a room's edit view also look like fields now, rather than plain text.
