## What's new

- Cards scroll under your finger again: on Home and in a room, swipe sideways and the row moves.
- Holding a machine's card, like the fridge, opens it, the same as any other card.
- After the hub updates, the wall picks up the new build on its own instead of waiting for a restart.
- The first card on Home stays level with its neighbors after the wall has rested.

## Details

On a touch screen, the cards on Home and in a room would not scroll sideways, while the rooms list
did. The cards had been told a finger on them may only move up and down, from a time when they
were laid out in a grid rather than a row. They now move with a finger in either direction. One
thing changes with that: sliding sideways across a light's card scrolls the row rather than
dimming the light. To dim, hold the card to open it and use the slider, or ask.

A machine with several parts, such as a fridge with an ice maker and a water dispenser, showed
its parts as rows but pressing and holding the card itself did nothing. It now opens the machine,
with its parts and a line saying how many are running. Holding a row still opens that part, and a
tap on a row still switches it.

After an update the hub came back on the new version but the wall kept showing the old panel until
somebody restarted it. The page now notices the hub has moved to a new build and reloads itself at
the moment the link comes back, never under a finger.

On a wall that had rested with something playing, the first card on Home came back a step below
the others and slightly smaller, and stayed that way until the page was reloaded. A card that goes
quiet from elsewhere fades off the row in steps, and one of those steps could land after the wall
had already closed the row over it, leaving a mark the next card for that speaker wore. It cannot
any more.
