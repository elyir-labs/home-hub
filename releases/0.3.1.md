## What's new

- Opening *This hub* looks for an update straight away, instead of waiting for the hub's next look a few hours on.

## Details

The hub looks for a newer build on its own every six hours. Opening *This hub*, on the wall or on a
phone, makes it look now: the "checked" time under the version is the answer, and the Install button
appears by itself if there is one. It will not ask again within five minutes of its last look, however
many times the page is opened, and a look that failed for want of internet counts as a look too.
