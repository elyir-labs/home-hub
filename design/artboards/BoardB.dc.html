A — in the room: the light says it, where you reach for it

The tile stops claiming a state it cannot have. It reads Staying off, with the reason in a few words under it and a small shield where the bulb was, in the lamp color: the strip is being looked after rather than broken.

The tap. A light that cannot come on cannot honor a tap, so on this one tile a tap opens why instead of doing nothing.

For — the fault is in the room and so is the fix. No new surface, no new sheet. And it is always true.
Against — quiet to the point of hiding. A roofline on a timer is in no room anybody opens.

Today — drawn from the shipped panel

The mock house’s kitchen, card for card where the shipped panel puts them at 1440 × 900, at dusk. The Under-cabinet strip reads On, 100%, warm, exactly as it would if it were lit.

It is not lit. Its controller was moved to a 24 V supply, the guard held the run off at boot, and it has said why every ten seconds since. The tile is the hub’s memory of what it asked for, and nothing tells it otherwise.
