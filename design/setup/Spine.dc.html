Sign in — when Home Assistant was already here
Someone set the engine up separately before the panel ever ran. It sits between welcome and the names, and it is not numbered: it is not a question about your house, it is a door being unlocked.
Building your home… — the wait after the names
The engine is still coming up. Centered, with the mark breathing, and it moves on by itself the moment the driver says ready. No number and no button, because there is nothing to answer.
The count is derived, not written down twice
One list in Setup.vue — ASKED = ['owner','code','location','rooms','devices'] — supplies both the position and the total. Adding a sixth question cannot leave a screen saying “of 5”, because nothing says 5.
Welcome carries no count, and that is the decision
It used to promise “one or two questions” and then ask five. A sentence that has to be rewritten every time ASKED grows or shrinks will quietly go out of date, so it now says “a few minutes, and you can change any of it later” — true at any length. The number of questions is free to change without touching a word of copy.
