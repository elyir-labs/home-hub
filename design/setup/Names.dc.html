the wiring, which is why this is now cheap
Last time I said folding these “re‑orders setupOwner and setPin”. Reading the brain: it mostly does not.
/setup/pin Pure settings. hub.lock.set() hashes and writes — no hub.ready() guard, no HA call. It can run before the engine exists. /setup/owner The only one that needs the driver (fresh | ready | connecting | needs-login), and the only one that takes time. It is what the wait is waiting for.
So one screen can take all four answers, set the passcode instantly, then start the engine and let the wait be the last thing on screen. There is no interleaving to get wrong.

the cost, and it is the whole argument
A keyboard takes about 40% of a phone and rather more of a wall tablet held in portrait. Two fields clear it. Four do not — the passcode boxes are under the keys, and the person is typing their name into a form they cannot see the end of.
The docked action row handles the button. It does not handle a field below the fold, because the thing that has to stay visible is the one being typed into.
C — what I would actually build, if you want the fold
One screen, two beats
The names, with the passcode revealed under them once a name is in — not a second screen and not a second step number. Nothing is hidden that has not already been earned, and at most two fields ever want the keyboard at once.
The field being typed into stays in view
The same ResizeObserver that docks the action row already knows the scroller’s height; a focused field scrolls itself into the space the keyboard leaves. This is the piece of work, and it is the only one.
And if that is too clever
Keep A. It is not broken — it is two calm screens and a wait, and the welcome no longer promises a number it has to keep. The fold is worth doing for the feel, not to fix anything.
