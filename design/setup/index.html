First run

The seven screens a person meets before they have a house — and the two that had their only button below the fold on the wall this product ships on. Drawn after the fact, from measurements taken in the browser, which is the wrong order and is why the collection exists.

Every board is 1440×900. The wall frames inside them are drawn at a real 1280×800 and then shrunk, so every measurement on a board is the measurement the panel actually has. Run the screens themselves with FRESH=1 PORT=8407 npm run dev:mock in app/, then ?setup=1&page=code.
