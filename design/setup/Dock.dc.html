Why sticky and not a fixed footer
A fixed footer is furniture: it is there on every screen whether it is needed or not, and it costs the same 80px on the one screen that had room for a fifth room chip. Sticky costs nothing until the content asks for it.
What re‑measures, and when
On mount, on resize, after a step transition lands, and whenever Adding grows a beat under the person’s hands — the one place in first run where a screen changes height while somebody is looking at it.
