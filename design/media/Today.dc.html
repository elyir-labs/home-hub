It asks in a server’s words. A folder path, a content type, which databases to download from. The household has a drive in a drawer and wants the films on it.
Its own people
Jellyfin keeps its own users and passwords. The hub already knows who lives here, and nobody should be asked to sign in to watch a film on their own TV.
Its own words when it fails
“This client isn’t compatible with the media and the server isn’t sending a compatible media format.” That is what a slow box says on a 4K film.
It does not know the house
Which room the TV is in, that the lights are on, that it is past bedtime for one of the people watching. The hub knows all of it.
What the hub has today
One card: the wall already shows what a TV is playing (design/player, A). There is no library, no phone screen, and nothing on the TV itself.
