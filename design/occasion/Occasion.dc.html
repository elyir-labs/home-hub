Why it is a chip and not a setting

Put Christmas on the strip and somebody has to go into each strip and set it, one at a time, and the bulbs and the switches that can do color never join in. Put it here and one tap does the house. The strip is not getting a feature; the house is, and the strip happens to render it best.

What the strip adds that a bulb cannot

Pixels, so an occasion on a strip is spatial — alternating along the run, or pooling at one end — where a bulb can only hold one color at a time. That is the whole of the strip's contribution, and it needs to know where the strip ends, which is the length question already answered.

The risk to hold on to

This gets used about three weeks a year. The failure mode is building an authoring screen for it. Ship a handful with household names, let “keep this” reuse the colors a room already remembers, and stop there.
