Six chips, and that is all of them

Two for the house, four for a room, and they share one idea between them: get ready to do a thing, or stop doing it. Watch a film. Go to bed. Leave.

Not one of them is about how the house feels, and none of them has a season. There is nowhere in this vocabulary for “it is December” to go.

The colors exist already, though — a room keeps the ones it has been set to (room_colors, api.py) and the swatches landed last week. What is missing is a reason to apply a set of them to the whole house at once.

The question this collection answers. A strip can do things a bulb cannot, and the obvious place to put them is the strip. This board exists to show that the obvious place is the wrong one: the bar above is where a household already goes to change how the whole house is.
