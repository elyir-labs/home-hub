The one question it adds, asked once, the way setup asks

The first routine that needs a direction lights one end of the strip and asks “What is this end nearest?” — the gate, the house. From then on “toward the house” means something, and nobody ever sees the word “reverse”.

Nothing here is a setting on the strip. A strip with no routine that uses it behaves exactly as it does today, and the effects test on its pane still holds.
