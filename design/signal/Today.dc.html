Every trigger a driveway needs exists: a gate is a door that opens, a car is motion, a phone is someone coming home. None of this collection is about noticing things.

The half that is missing — “then”, for a light

Every outcome a routine has is a state: the light ends up somewhere and stays. What the driveway wants is an event — something that happens along the strip, means one thing, and is over.

And the strip has never been told which end is which

Setup asks two things — how long, and which order the colors come in. A strip that runs toward something needs a third: which end is the house. Nothing asks it today, because until now nothing needed it.
