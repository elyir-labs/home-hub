C — the house has a few things it can say

The meaning is the house’s. The strip just shows it best.

This house
What the lights tell you

A few things worth knowing without looking at a screen. Each one shows on the lights near where it is happening, then they go back to how they were.

Someone’s arrivingThe drive shows the way to the door · after dark
Someone’s leavingThe drive shows the way to the gate · after dark
A door’s been left openLights in the room it opens off, until it is shut
Something’s nearly doneThe car charging, the wash · lights in the room it is in

Four, drawn by us, and the list does not grow from this screen. A household that wants its own goes to Routines, which is where a sentence becomes a rule.

“Someone’s arriving”, on each thing that can say it
Driveway strip
Runs toward the house — it has a length and knows its ends
Porch bulb
One color, no length — so it breathes
Hall light
White only — comes up to half, if it was off, and says so on the room
The wall
One line in the band: “Someone’s at the gate”
Where “arriving” comes from

From the house, once, not from each strip: a phone coming home, a gate opening, the order two things fire in. Knowing in from out is the hard part of this whole idea, and here it is solved in one place — and wrong in one place when it is wrong.
