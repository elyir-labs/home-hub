Not words here: meteor, fire, rainbow, strobe. Asked for one, the house answers with the five it has.

For

It is what the household already does, in the words they used. The grammar is small and fixed — colors, one of five words, slowly or quickly — so the house reads it without a model, the way simple commands stay deterministic.

Nothing is picked from a list, so there is no list to become a zoo; and the draft plays on the roof before it is kept, so keeping it is keeping something seen.

Against

Two of the five, flickering and twinkling, are new, and a vocabulary is a menu you have to remember instead of read. A household that never thinks to type has no way in but the shipped occasions.

So C is never alone: it is how a look is changed, not how one arrives. And every look typed is one more thing the house keeps, per occasion, for years.
