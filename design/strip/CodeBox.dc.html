The one everybody else already does.

Every Matter device in the shop works this way, because the standard requires it: the advertisement carries a discriminator and never the passcode, so something has to bring the passcode from outside the radio. A number on the label is how Apple, Google, Hue and IKEA all solve it, and a household has met it before.

For

Honest, standard and finished. It works in anybody’s app, not just ours, so the strip a household buys is the same object whether or not they own our hub. Nothing has to be kept secret by us, nothing can leak from our manufacturing, and there is no algorithm anybody can work out.

Against

It gives up the thing design/puck/Knock.dc.html was proudest of. That board’s whole argument is that the identity check is the object and never a number — no chip id, no serial, nothing to read out to somebody. Here the wall asks for eleven digits before it will do anything, and our panel is then no better at this than the app that came with anybody else’s strip.
