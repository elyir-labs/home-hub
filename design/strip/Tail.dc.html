You are looking for an edge. So show an edge, and nothing else.

The strip goes dark except for the last handful of lights, in a color it is not otherwise wearing. Shorter and longer walk that bright tail along the wire. At sixty lights to the metre a lit strip is a glow and its end is a guess; a short bright tail on a dark strip is unmistakable, from across the room and from under a shelf.
