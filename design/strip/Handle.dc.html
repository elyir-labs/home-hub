The readout is not decoration, and that is the problem

Dragging past the real end shows nothing at all — the surplus falls off the wire — so the picture alone cannot tell you where you are. The number is there to cover the direction you cannot see. Its own board said so: “that readout is the tell: it is there because the picture alone does not close the question.”

For

One movement, whatever the size of the mistake, and it is the pane’s existing gesture rather than a new one. Nothing is timed; you can take as long as you like and the strip goes on saying the same thing.

Against

It is a search in the dark in one direction, and it puts a measurement on the wall in a product whose whole argument is that you never take one. It is also the worst control for the actual complaint: moving one light with a finger on a 400 px track is three lights per pixel.

Worth saying plainly: this board is not wrong, it is aimed elsewhere. It was drawn for the household who cut a metre off, and it is very good at that. The report it is being asked to answer is “a bit less or more than it actually is”.
