Stripes mean it carries a separate white as well, which is worth knowing and is not a fault. Nothing at all is a fault, and goes somewhere else.

Two primaries settle all six orderings, because whichever color comes back names the channel that went out first. Red is asked here because the common strip is already right and answers in one tap; a second question only ever reaches the households whose first answer was no. Getting it wrong harms nothing — it is the wrong color, not a damaged strip — so the way back lives on the tile, which is where the second strip somebody buys will need it anyway.
