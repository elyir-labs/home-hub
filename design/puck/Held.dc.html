When the code is behind the wall plate.

A switch in the wall has its code screwed to its own back, and nobody takes a plate off to add a light to a phone. The mesh never required that code — every switch on this house’s network was claimed without one. What it really proves is that this is the switch in front of you, and without it that proof is your finger.
