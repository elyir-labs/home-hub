Which one has the light?

Two switches on one light are not the same as each other: one is wired to the lamp and the other is wired to nothing at all, and the house has to write that down before it can pair them. Coming across from the old panel it can be read. Out of a box it cannot — so the house finds out by trying it, and only asks if trying it was inconclusive. The words “load” and “companion” never appear.
