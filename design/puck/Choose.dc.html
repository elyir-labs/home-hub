Keep the panel, or leave it behind.

It looks like a fork and it is really an order. A working panel does not have to be touched at all — the house can work your switches alongside it, today, with nothing reset and nothing rewired. Bringing them over properly is the better end state and it can happen later, a light at a time. Only somebody whose panel has already died has one road, and they are not being asked to choose anything.
