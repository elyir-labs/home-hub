The panel is also a light switch.

On the wall the old panel is also a slider somebody reaches for: one of these lights has three switches and the panel is one of them. Taking it down removes a switch position — and orphans a second switch that was talking to it. Say both before anyone picks up a screwdriver, and fix the half that can be fixed.

2 · the half the house can fix
what would happen on its own

One of the wall switches sends its press to the panel. With the panel gone it would be a plate that does nothing — the light would answer at one switch only.

what it does instead

It points that switch at the one the lamp is wired to instead. One thing written, once, before the panel comes off — and then the two of them talk to each other with nothing in the middle.
