C — dark, unless something is wrong.

A bridge for strips is not placed like one for switches. It goes where a strip needs it, and the strip’s own setup is where that is known: when the hub cannot hear a strip well, that screen asks for a bridge between them and says when it can hear it. The bridge itself only speaks up when it cannot reach the hub.

What the light says

Breathing red It cannot reach the hub. Try a socket nearer the router.
A soft glow Only if you asked for a nightlight.

For

Placing is done at the moment it matters, on the screen that already knows the answer, and the bridge needs no new message. A bridge that is fine is quiet, which is what furniture should be.

Against

No moment of ‘leave it here’ in your hand: the answer is on a screen, which is the thing the switch bridge’s placing board argued against. A bridge bought before any strip has nothing to say at all.
