B — green means it can hear the strip it is for.

Green answers the question the person is actually asking: will this fix my strip? The hub knows which strips it cannot hear well, so it tells the bridge which ones are its job, and the light goes green when the bridge can hear them and reach the hub. In a house with no strips yet, reaching the hub is enough.

For

The light keeps the meaning it has today: the thing you are placing it for can be heard. Nobody leaves a bridge green and useless by the router.

Against

The bridge cannot know which strips are its job; the hub has to tell it, which is a new message to the bridge. A house with two bridges has to decide which strip each one is for, and a strip that moves changes the answer.
