A 12 V strip with a spare data wire

Many 12 V strips carry a backup data wire so one dead light does not darken the rest. That is four wires, and it still takes its colors on one. Counted honestly, it answers Four, is sent a clock it does not have, and is as dark as it is today — with the household now sure they answered wrong.

And our sockets are sealed, with tails made to fit whatever plug a strip arrives with. Plenty of people will look and see a plug, not wires.

For: nothing is timed, nothing has to light, and it is answered before the first color is ever shown. Against: the count is a proxy for the thing we want, and the proxy is wrong for a common strip. It also asks somebody to look at the one part of the install we told them not to worry about — the plug.
