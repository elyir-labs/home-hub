The case
A household thinks room by room, and a room is the only container that already holds a lamp, a sensor with no tile, a light strip and a wall switch at the same time. It is far and away the least new code — the screen exists, the two-tap ask exists and is good — and the words on it are already the best words anywhere in this feature.
Against
Ending a thing stays two screens away from the thing. “What’s in here” is a mode you enter and leave, and the panel has almost none of those. And a puck and an account are still somewhere else, so this does not close the question either.

If only one of the three gets built, build this one. It turns the report from “there is no way” into “there is a way, and it is signposted”.
