This is where somebody who wants a thing gone will look, because it is the thing. The verb is called Rename or move it, and that is exactly and only what it does. Forget is not here, and nothing here says where Forget is.

And three with no way out at all
A light strip
No route, and nothing to build one on
There is no forget anywhere in /strip: Radio.forget() at strip.py:374 is return None, with no callers. Forgetting its device through the pencil drops it from the house and leaves it adopted in the hub — half out, and nothing says so.
What it needs: a forget that tells the strip to forget the house too, so it can be set up again — here or in somebody else’s.
A Brilliant wall switch
Answers Forget, and is back by morning
The puck republishes discovery every MQTT session (announced, main.cpp:171), so the row returns the next time it reconnects. The mesh node keeps our netkey either way: the only real way back is a factory reset at the wall.
What it needs: a forget the puck keeps across a reboot, and honors by staying quiet — with letting the switch in again as the way back.
A device its account owns
Refused, correctly, into a dead end
“It goes when the account that brought it does” is true, and the right sentence to say. The panel then leaves you standing on the row with no way through to the account door it has just named.
What it needs: the door, where the sentence is said. Which screen that lands on is the one part of this that waits for the choice.
