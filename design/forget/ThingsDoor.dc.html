This is also the screen somebody actually wants when they are moving house, selling a camera, or wondering what on earth is still signed in — which is the test of whether a list is an inventory or a settings panel.
The case
It is the only direction that can hold all of it: a lamp, a sensor with no tile, a light strip, a wall switch on a mesh, a puck, an account. Every other direction leaves two or three of those somewhere else.

The grouping is not decoration. What brought a thing is exactly what decides whether it may leave alone — the brain says so in its own words today and the panel has nowhere to draw it. Here the answer is the row: Goes with Ring, with the door to Ring on the group above it.
Against
It is a new screen, and the most code of the three.

It is also an inventory, and an inventory is close to the thing section 5 says the box is not for: a list of everything, in one place, that reads like a settings panel. Drawn badly it becomes Home Assistant’s device page with nicer type.

And it is still not where the person is looking. Somebody holding the tile for a lamp they just unscrewed has to walk to This house to end it.

Which is why C and A are not exclusive. The door is the place everything can be reached; the row on the pane is the shortcut for the thing in your hand.
