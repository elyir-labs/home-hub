For

It works, today, unchanged. A whole adoption went down this exact ladder and a strip joined the household’s network, with the hub never in radio range of it. The puck read none of it.

Against

A hundred and seventy round trips for one bit of news. The wait is 95% of the traffic; it is what exhausts NimBLE’s buffers mid-session (rc=6, which reads as a dropped link and is not one); and it holds the second radio open the whole time somebody is walking across the house. Everything else on this row argues about this band.
