For

The strip barely changes — it answers nothing instead of “waiting” — and the hub sends one request rather than a hundred and seventy. The puck polls at whatever rate suits its other job, so the mesh gets to choose, which the hub two rooms away cannot. A dropped poll is retried in the hall instead of becoming a lost errand.

Against

It hands the courier a bit it did not have: that something happened, from a length rather than content. Small, arguably inferable from timing anyway, and still the first hole in “the corridor knows nothing”. It also keeps the GATT chatter that exhausts the buffers and merely moves it off the hub’s clock, so the pool still has to be sized for a two-minute wait.
