For

The wait is nearly free — one ask every ten seconds as a backstop instead of every 0.7, and the ring cuts it short the moment a finger lands. The ring carries no content and the answer still comes back inside the session, so the tube is exactly as sealed as it was. Measured: seven exchanges pressed at once, fourteen after a ninety-second wait.

Against

It needs firmware on the strip, which one already in a house will not have — that strip is simply polled the old way. And holding the link is what costs, not asking: the puck’s own mesh polls pile up while two links share its radio, so the puck must hold them back while an errand runs. It does now, and the pool sat at 32 of 100 through a ninety-second wait instead of reaching zero.
