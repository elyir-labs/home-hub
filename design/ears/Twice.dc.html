For

No link is held while somebody walks across the house, so the second radio is free, the mesh is untouched, and the buffer pool comes back at every close — measured, not hoped for. The second session needs no scan: the strip’s address holds for the whole boot. And a puck that dies during the wait costs nothing, because nothing was in flight.

Against

The press becomes visible to anybody in range. The gate is still inside the session, so what a stranger gains is knowing somebody pressed a button — which a lit strip through a window also tells them. Small, but a real change to what the object broadcasts, and it should be chosen rather than slid into. And SRP6a runs twice: 2.5 seconds, and a second place an adoption can fail half-done.
