C — the glass already in the room does it

The wall is in the living room, it has a Bluetooth radio, and it is already the panel. No new hardware at all.

Web Bluetooth in the browser the panel is already running in. The hub still holds the session; the glass in the room is the radio. It is the cheapest answer on this canvas — and it is the only one that makes the household meet the machinery, because the browser will not hand a page a radio without putting its own chooser in front of it first.

This is the screen the whole of design/door/ was drawn to avoid. Two strips in a box are PROV_52e20 and PROV_2e425, the addresses rotate between scans so the row is different every time, and the household is being asked to identify their own light by a string. The object was supposed to be the identity: it is the one that is lit.
Three things that have to be true, and none has been tried here
  • ×A secure context. Web Bluetooth is refused outside one, and the panel is served to the wall over plain http:// at an address on the LAN. Our certificates are per hub on elyir.app and that is the away path, not this one.
  • ×A browser that has the API. The wall is an Android WebView inside kiosk/, not Chrome, and Web Bluetooth is not a WebView API.
  • ×A person standing at the glass. A gesture per connection is the permission model, not a setting — so this cannot run at three in the morning, and it cannot run at all from a phone in another house.
And a phone, which clears the first two

Chrome on somebody’s phone, over the hub’s own certificate on elyir.app, is a secure context in a browser that has the API. It is still a chooser and still a tap, and it makes a phone a thing setup depends on.

For

Nothing to buy and nothing to ship. The one screen a household already has is usually in the room they are lighting, and it costs no firmware anywhere.

Against

It sells the product’s one idea for a radio. Three unknowns, an unskippable chooser full of vocabulary we have spent this whole design deleting, and a setup that only happens where somebody is standing. Keep it drawn, as the rung for a house with a wall and nothing else — not as the answer.
