Ears

How a hub in the garage reaches a light strip in the living room, when Bluetooth will not cross the house. Whatever hears the strip is a courier: the session stays end to end and the press gate stays on the device, so these boards argue only about who the courier is.

  • Today — one radio, and it is in the garage. The measured thirteen decibels, and the sentence that ships instead.
  • A · Errand — the bridge puck runs the errand. Nothing on the wall changes.
  • B · Anyone — every powered thing of ours is an ear, and the hub asks the loudest.
  • C · InHand — the wall panel over Web Bluetooth, and the chooser it cannot skip.
  • Deaf — the failure all three end at, in one sentence. Not a fourth direction.

The canvas and the notes that argue each board are in canvas.json; open them with tools/dev.sh design. The problem is written up in docs/strip.md, items 15 and 25.
