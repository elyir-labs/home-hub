The rule

A tile never shows an empty frame waiting for a picture that will not arrive. Every rung fills the same 236 × 190 with something intentional, so a shelf of no-name plugs looks composed rather than broken.

Rung 4 is what most houses will mostly see. If it does not hold up on its own, the design does not hold up.
