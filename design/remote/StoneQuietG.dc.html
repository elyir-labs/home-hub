G / The stone: the ring follows what is playing

No keys for the sound: the ring is the volume while a film or music plays, and the lights the rest of the time. A Favorite key on each side, for either hand, runs the room's favorite scene, and held while the ring turns it is always the lights.

Nothing playing: turning the ring dims the lamps in the room it is in. Pressing OK turns them off.

Hold Favorite and turn for the lights while music plays. It works the same with anything playing.

For it
  • The calmest stone: four things on top, one key on each side, mirrored for either hand.
  • One rule for the ring, and one way to reach the lights that always works.
Against it
  • What the ring turns depends on whether something is playing.
  • Holding Favorite to turn the lights is never found by accident; it has to be told once.
To find out
  • Whether holding a side key while turning the ring is comfortable in one hand.
  • Whether two side keys get pressed when the stone is picked up.
Drawn at 3.4 px to the millimeter. The keys on its sides sit on its widest belt, where the stone is about 12 mm tall. Presses while it is being picked up are ignored.
