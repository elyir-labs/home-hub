H / The ring sits in a shallow dish, and is 42 mm across

The ring and pad come down 2.7 mm on shorter caps, as low as the pad's tilt allows, and the top dips gently into a dish around them, so the thumb rests in it. The ring is 42 mm across instead of 46, round the same 30 mm pad, so it reaches less far down the dome. Nothing on the board moves. The stone's highest point drops from 24 mm to about 22, at the dish's rim.

  • From above, a soft shadow round the ring: the dish.
  • The thumb finds the ring by feel and rests there.
  • Along the stone the ring sits 0.3 mm under the dish's rim. Across it, where the dome falls fastest, it stands 0.5 mm proud at the sides, against 4 mm today and 1.3 with the 46 mm ring.
For it
  • A place for the thumb, which is where it lives.
  • The step is gone at the ends and half a millimeter at the sides.
  • A little slimmer, and no board change.
Against it
  • The peak is gone: from the side, the middle sits a little below the shoulders.
  • The ring is 5.5 mm wide to grip instead of 7.5.
  • It is as low as the ring goes: the pad's tilt keeps 1.3 mm under its edge, on a thinner ring.
