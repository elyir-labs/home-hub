Elyir / Our own remote / today

Today / A bought remote, plugged into the hub

A typical remote, its receiver plugged into the hub. Most keys pass to the streaming box while it is on screen; Home and the colored keys bring the house back. It knows nothing of the room, and with the TV off it does nothing.

TV off, late
Nothing happens

Every key does nothing. The remote talks to what is on the screen, and nothing is. The lamps are dimmed from a phone or the wall.

A film on

The streaming box gets the arrows. Home or a colored key brings the room up. Dimming is Up, Up, Up, one step a press.

Carried to the bedroom
Still the living room, if it reaches

It has no idea it moved. It still talks to the hub by the living room TV, as far as its receiver can hear it.

What it does well
  • It is here this week, it is cheap, and the hub already listens to it (the work on the streaming box keys).
  • Everyone already knows how to hold it.
What it cannot do
  • Nothing without the screen. No room of its own, no level but pressing, no way to talk to the house, no way to find it.
  • Half its keys mean nothing here.
What it can teach us
  • Count which keys get pressed in the living room over a few weeks. That sets which keys ours keeps.
Drawn as a typical one; the one ordered is not here yet. The keys the hub uses are the ones on the tv-remote branch (passed to an Apple TV; other boxes keep their own remote). Not yet tried on the real Apple TV.
