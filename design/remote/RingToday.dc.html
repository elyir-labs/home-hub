Today / The ring as the parts put it

A flat ring on a curved top. The ring and pad stand at the dome's peak, so around the ring's edge the dome has already fallen away: the ring stands proud by about 1.5 mm along the stone and nearly 4 mm across it.

  • From above it reads as drawn.
  • From the side, and under a thumb, it is a dial set on a pebble rather than part of it.
  • Its edge, most of all on the left and right, is the first thing a pocket or a sofa cushion catches.
For it
  • Nothing to change: the caps print as they are.
Against it
  • A step of up to 3.8 mm all round the ring, highest at the sides.
  • It looks fitted on, not made as one.
What it shows
  • Any answer has to deal with the dome falling faster across the stone than along it.
Cut through the center of the ring, drawn at 6 px to the millimeter. The shell is the stand-in from the board's height check (an 11 mm upright side meeting a dome, 24 mm at its peak); the board, the battery under it and the ring's part are where the board puts them.
