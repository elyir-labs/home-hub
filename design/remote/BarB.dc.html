B / The bar: a remote you already know how to hold

Long and slim, held and pointed like every remote in every house. Its ring turns as well as presses, so brighter, dimmer and louder are one turn. Home is the house. Four keys carry the room's scenes with the TV off, and a key on top listens while it is held.

Hold, say it, let go. The same sentences the house already understands when typed. With the TV off, the light blinks once for done.

For it
  • Nobody has to learn to hold it. It points, it fits a hand, it fits the gap in the sofa.
  • Everything a streaming box asks for, plus the house: dimming in one turn and scenes in one press, TV on or off.
Against it
  • Twelve things to press is the most of the three, and the most that can mean different things.
  • From across the room it looks like any other remote. What makes it ours is what it does.
To find out
  • A ring that turns around a four-way press is the one part to find before anything else is drawn.
  • Whether four scene keys are read in the dark, or only by color in daylight.
