What a long press opens today

Held a tile of every kind in the running panel and measured what rose. Nine kinds, one layout, one watermark.

The same four measurements came back for every kind held: the pane at 0, 225, 1440 × 675, the name at 49, 295, the reading at 49, 475, and between 131 and 349px of pane with nothing in it. The watermark behind is 260px, drawn from the same nine-icon set whatever was opened.
What the pane said, kind by kind
Ceiling light 35% Turn off The one instrument that exists.
Living room TV Playing Turn off Artwork, position, transport: dropped.
Thermostat 74° Turn on No way to set the temperature.
Blinds 70% Turn on “cover cannot on”: the button errors.
Front door locked Turn on The raw state string. Same error.
Backyard cam recording Turn on No picture until you press Watch.
Robot mower docked Turn on Same error. A mower has no on.
Motion, Temperature, Back door — — No tile, so nothing to hold.
Four of the nine kinds offer a control the brain refuses: act(id, ‘on’) has no entry in SERVICE for a lock, a cover, a camera or a vacuum, so the one big button on those panes raises a 400 and puts an error across the screen.
