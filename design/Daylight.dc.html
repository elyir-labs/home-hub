Three rules, and one that stays put

Lightness is relative — a tile is always +0.135 above the field (Pastel, +0.335). It can never invert.

Chroma falls as the sky gets colorful — 0.050 against a near-gray night, 0.034 against midday blue. Quiet tiles under a loud sky.

Hue stays absolute. Warmth is a place on the wheel, not a relationship — rotating it off the sky turned Warm pink at night.

The lamp accent never moves. On means #e9b872 at every hour, in every tone. It is a signal, not decoration — same for the live green and the danger red.
