Knock

How the house says something new is here, and how you go looking on purpose. A knock takes the whole screen today and can be a hundred and eight seconds late — and the panel already has a politer way of saying the same thing, which it uses for everything except a knock.

  • Today — the same event, two manners, both shipping; and where every second of the delay is.
  • A · Never — an arrival is news, news goes in the band, the sheet is what a tap opens.
  • B · Witnessed — it takes the screen only when the arrival came through the hub itself.
  • C · OnAdd — it takes the page only where somebody is already asking.
  • Look — Add as a thing you do rather than a place you wait. Every direction needs it.
