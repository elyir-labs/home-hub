The rule, in one line

A thing that arrives is news. News goes in the band. The sheet is what a tap opens, and it is never what an event opens.

For

It makes the 108 seconds harmless. A chip that turns up a minute late is a chip; a screen that is taken a minute late is a fault. It is also the only answer with no rule to get wrong when the fifth product ships, and it is already built — one more line in the band the panel already draws.

Against

It charges a tap for something that was already deliberate. Somebody who has just pushed a puck into the hub’s own socket did the plugging in as the act of setting it up, and the house answers by putting a line on a screen in another room. And the very first device in a new house is being added by somebody who has never seen this band and does not know it is where to look.

Nothing about the chip is new except its text. The 26px pill, the 32px lamp-colored icon, the title at 15px and the subtitle at 13.5px are panel.css’s .nudge, unchanged — which is the point: the panel has had the right manner for a network arrival all along and gave a knock a different one.
The chip is not the only quiet thing. The + door in the bar carries an 8px lamp dot whenever something is waiting — which is what remains after an hour, when the chip folds in with anything else waiting. The house stops talking; it does not forget. Not mine keeps the meaning _dismissed already gives it: gone until the thing is power-cycled.
