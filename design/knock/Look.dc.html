What this buys, beyond the tap

One list, whatever door a thing came to. A Bluetooth knock and a thing on the Wi‑Fi are the same sentence to a household — something new, not set up yet — and today they are two unrelated mechanisms with two different manners. Here they are rows in one list.

And it says why something is missing. The nearest honest thing the house can say when a strip is not in the list is that nothing could hear it, which is design/ears/Deaf.dc.html’s sentence arriving where somebody is actually looking for it.

The two changes pay for each other

Scanning here means the background loop can be quietened right down, or stopped. watch(every=20.0) exists to catch an event its own comment describes as happening “when somebody plugs a thing in and is standing right there” — which is this page. So the radio gets cheaper all day and the moment that matters gets faster.

How long a chip lives — decided 22 September

A strip knocks for forty-eight hours (item 17), and a chip that will not go away is the interruption again, slower. So: its own line for an hour, then it folds in with anything else waiting — 2 things waiting to be set up — and it goes when the thing stops knocking. After the hour the + door still carries its dot, so the house stops talking without forgetting. Not mine keeps _dismissed’s meaning: gone until it is power-cycled.
